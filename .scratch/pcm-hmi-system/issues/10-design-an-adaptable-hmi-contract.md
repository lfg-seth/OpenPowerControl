# Design an adaptable HMI contract

## Question

What HMI event/state model lets the first 12-position SPDT on-off-on panel work well while allowing future toggles, momentaries, rotary controls, LEDs, and small displays without embedding vehicle wiring or PCM channel numbers in the input-scanning code?

Prototype representative mappings, including maintained three-position controls, momentary mode selection, mutual exclusion, status/fault feedback, reconnect/resynchronization, and a future alternate HMI presenting the same vehicle functions.
