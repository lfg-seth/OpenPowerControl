# Define the safe operating envelope

## Question

For every important failure or transition—cold boot, brownout, watchdog reset, corrupted configuration, CAN silence, bus-off, HMI disconnect, node-ID conflict, stuck switch, overcurrent, overtemperature, sensor failure, and partial PCM failure—what outputs are allowed, which must turn off, which may retain state, what must be latched, and what operator indication or recovery is required?

The answer must distinguish ordinary accessories, compressor-related loads, steady road lighting, and programmable warning-light functions, and must identify any functions that require independent hardware interlocks rather than firmware-only safeguards.
